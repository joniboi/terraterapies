Yes. And I'll be quite honest, because I think there is **a good website underneath this, but the landing page is not yet giving the business the level of premium impression that the physical centre seems to deserve.**

I'm judging this from the screenshot you gave me, not from the implementation/code.

## My overall impression

If I arrive at this page as a potential customer, I get:

> **"This is a professional massage/wellness centre, with a nice Asian/premium aesthetic and a lot of different treatments."**

That's good.

But I don't immediately get:

> **"Wow, this place looks beautiful. I want to go there."**

And that's the gap I'd try to close.

The biggest problem isn't that the design is ugly. **It isn't.** The individual elements are actually quite nice.

The problem is **presentation, hierarchy and scale**.

# 2. The photographs are actually one of your strongest assets

This is something I think you're underusing.

You've shown me photographs of:

- the entrance/logo
- the massage room
- the corridor with candles
- the treatment rooms
- the reception
- the therapists
- the Buddha/decorative elements

And the physical place looks **much more luxurious and atmospheric than the homepage currently communicates.**

The customer should almost feel:

before they even read about a treatment.

The corridor photo, for example, is fantastic for this purpose.

The treatment-room photographs also have a much stronger emotional effect than generic category cards.

# 3. Your hero is too weak

This is probably the second thing I'd attack.

The top of the page currently gives me:

> Lotus de Bali & Thai
> Massage...
> [Reserva ahora] [Regala un tratamiento]

But the hero doesn't seem to **sell the experience** strongly enough.

For a business like this, the first screen should communicate three things almost instantly:

### WHERE

**Sarrià – Sant Gervasi, Barcelona**

### WHAT

**Masajes balineses y tailandeses · Rituales · Faciales**

### WHY

**Un espacio para desconectar y cuidar de ti.**

And then:

**RESERVAR CITA**

That should be extremely obvious.

At the moment, the page makes me start navigating the catalogue before it has really seduced me.

# 4. "Nuestros Masajes" is definitely wrong now that I've seen the page

You noticed this yourself indirectly.

The section contains:

- Bonos
- Faciales
- Rituales
- Ritual en Pareja
- Orientales
- Relajantes
- Reafirmantes
- Terapéutico relajante

So:

> **Nuestros Masajes**

is conceptually incorrect.

I'd change it to something like:

### **Descubre nuestros tratamientos**

And underneath:

> Masajes, rituales y tratamientos faciales diseñados para tu bienestar.

That immediately makes the whole section coherent.

# 5. The category cards are attractive, but there are too many levels competing

This is the most interesting design problem.

You currently have something like:

### BIG cards

Bono de sesiones | Faciales
Rituales | Ritual en Pareja

### SMALL cards

Orientales | Relajantes | Reafirmantes | Terapéutico relajante

I understand the logic.

But visually it says:

> "Here are eight equally important things."

They're not equally important.

I'd create a much stronger hierarchy.

### Primary experiences

**Masajes**
**Rituales**
**Faciales**
**Experiencias para parejas**

Then inside **Masajes**:

> Orientales · Relajantes · Terapéuticos · Reafirmantes

And separately:

> **Bonos de sesiones**

This would make the page much easier to understand.

# 6. I would NOT put all the treatments on the homepage

This is important given what we've just been discussing with Google.

The homepage shouldn't become:

> Balinés
> Tailandés
> Shiatsu
> Quiromasaje
> Lomi-Lomi
> Craneofacial
> Piedras calientes
> etc.

That's what your treatment catalogue is for.

The homepage's job is:

**seduce → explain → establish trust → make booking easy.**

Then the customer explores the catalogue.

Your current approach is actually fairly close to this. I'd just make the hierarchy stronger.

---

# 7. The "reviews" section needs to be much more important

You have:

> **Lo que dicen nuestros clientes**

but visually it is relatively small and low on the page.

For a massage business, reviews are **conversion ammunition**.

A person who doesn't know Lotus de Bali is thinking:

> Is this place actually good?

Three excellent reviews answer that.

I'd make this section more visually prominent.

For example:

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

**"Una experiencia maravillosa..."**

> "..."

**— María, Google**

And then 3 cards.

Even better if you can eventually integrate the actual Google rating/review count.

---

# 8. The website currently feels more like a catalogue than a luxury experience

This is probably my most important conceptual criticism.

Your **physical centre** appears to be selling:

> tranquillity
> atmosphere
> personal attention
> exoticism
> relaxation
> premium experience

But the homepage currently communicates more:

> categories
> buttons
> treatments
> navigation

I'd shift the balance.

### Current feeling

**"Here is our menu of services."**

### What I'd want

**"Come and experience Lotus de Bali & Thai."**

Then the menu comes afterwards.

That's a subtle but important difference.

---

# 9. The orange CTA is slightly disconnected from the visual identity

From the screenshot, the **"Ver más"** buttons are orange.

It isn't terrible, but your brand is visually much more:

- black
- gold
- warm beige
- dark brown
- candlelight
- natural/wood tones

I'd probably make the CTA system more consistent with that.

For example:

**Gold / warm ochre accent + black**

rather than a fairly bright orange.

Not because orange is objectively wrong, but because the physical centre you've shown me has a very sophisticated **gold + dark + warm neutral** aesthetic.

The website should reproduce that feeling.

---

# 10. The footer is too small

Again, this is related to the scale problem.

The footer contains useful things:

- experience
- collaborations
- information
- location
- social media

But everything becomes tiny.

I'd give it more breathing room and make the essential information more readable.

Especially:

**📍 Sarrià-Sant Gervasi, Barcelona**

**📞 632 14 17 62**

**🕐 Horarios**

**📅 Reservar cita**

That's information a potential customer may actually need.

# What I would NOT do

I would **not redesign the whole thing**.

That's important.

The current site already has:

- coherent branding
- good photography
- nice category imagery
- clear navigation
- booking CTA
- gift-card CTA
- reviews
- treatment catalogue
- responsive structure
- multilingual support

You've built the foundations correctly.

I'd call this a **refinement project, not a rebuild**.
